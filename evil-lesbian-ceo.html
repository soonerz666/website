EVIL LESBIAN CEO

EVIL LESBIAN CEO was a satirical comedy I wrote and performed at Black Spark Cultural Centre for the 2024 Melbourne Fringe Festival — soundtracked almost entirely by Tinashe.

The show questioned the motives of out lesbian and Woodside CEO, Meg O’Neil—whose company is behind Australia’s biggest and most polluting fossil fuel venture, the North West Shelf Project.

It dared ask what nobody else would: can a CEO frack the planet and still be part of the LGBTQIA+ community?

A portion of ticket sales was donated to the Disrupt Burrup Hub campaign to support activists with court costs and fines. Check out the campaign here.
