Star Gazing!

A celestial shindig at Cuppabumbalong Homestead in Thawra (ACT), all happening under the Milky Way. We gave folks constellation cards, sky-charts, and compasses to help them navigate the cosmos.

The sold out event was capped at 160 attendees over two nights to ensure COVID-19 distancing requirements were met. Woodfired pizza and beer provided by Capital Brewing Co. fueled the night, accompanied by a live musical performance of star-themed songs. And for those wanting to extend the experience, overnight camping was available right on the homestead.

The constellation cards were designed on Photoshop and developed to guide attendees through the Southern Hemisphere's major constellations, offering tips and tricks for finding them on the night.

We were pretty lucky to be blessed with two perfectly clear nights (there was, foolishly, no Plan B) for Milky Way spotting, and even saw many a wombat roaming under the stars.
